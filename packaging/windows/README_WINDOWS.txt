Aloha! Welcome to Tiki Karaoke Lounge, the turnkey, full-venue karaoke 
and party entertainment system for Windows.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. QUICK START GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. INSTALLER EDITION (TikiKaraokeSetup-Pro-1.0.6.exe / Free):
   1. Double-click the installer executable to install.
   2. Launch "Tiki Karaoke Lounge" from your Desktop or Start Menu.
   3. The desktop Control Hub and setup wizard will open automatically.

B. PORTABLE ZIP EDITION (TikiKaraoke-Pro-Windows-v1.0.6-Portable.zip):
   1. Extract the ZIP folder anywhere on your computer (e.g., C:\TikiKaraoke).
   2. Double-click TikiKaraoke.exe to launch. No administrative installation required!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS & HARDWARE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- OS: Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit)
- Display: 1 or 2 Displays (Dual-monitor recommended: Laptop/Host screen + TV Stage display)
- Audio: Built-in speakers, HDMI/TV audio, or external PA system / USB Audio Interface
- Microphone: Any USB microphone, wireless receiver, or line-in interface
- Network: Local Wi-Fi router (Internet access required for YouTube song downloads)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW IT WORKS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

📺 1. THE STAGE DISPLAY (TV / Projector):
   - Opens automatically on your secondary monitor in borderless fullscreen.
   - Plays song videos with scrolling lyrics.
   - Displays a dynamic QR code for singers to scan with their smartphones.
   - Shows "NOW SINGING" and "UP NEXT" singer tickers.
   - Automatically plays intermission music videos or sing-alongs when queue is idle!

📱 2. SINGER SMARTPHONE JOIN:
   - Guests scan the QR code on the TV stage with any smartphone camera.
   - Zero app download required! Works instantly in mobile Safari, Chrome, Edge.
   - Singers choose their name and tropical avatar (🌺, 🌴, 🍹, 🦜, 🏄).
   - Search tens of thousands of karaoke songs and queue with custom key/pitch adjustments.

👑 3. PRIVATE HOST DESK:
   - Access from your host laptop or from your phone.
   - Play, pause, restart, or skip songs on the fly.
   - Re-order queue, manage duet singers, or moderate singer names.
   - Adjust microphone volume, warm room reverb, and between-song audio ducking.
   - Choose your Intermission Vibe Mode:
     • 📼 Pure Filler (Official music videos)
     • 🎤 True Karaoke (Crowd sing-alongs with lyrics & vocals)
     • 🧠 Smart Mashup (Sing-alongs when empty, music videos when paused)

🧹 4. SYSTEM TRAY CONTROL:
   - Right-click the Tiki icon in your Windows notification tray anytime to:
     • Open Stage Display
     • Open Host DJ Remote
     • Open Setup & Audio Configuration
     • Copy Public / Singer Join URL
     • Cleanly shut down all background services

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. TROUBLESHOOTING & VENUE TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Windows Firewall: When prompted on first launch, check "Allow access on Private networks".
- Offline Karaoke: All played songs are permanently cached locally. You can pre-download
  starter crates from the Host Desk -> BGM Crate tab.
- Audio Latency / Delay: If your venue TV introduces HDMI audio lag, adjust the 
  "A/V Sync Latency" slider on the Host Desk or Setup page.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. MEDIA IMPORT & CURATED OFFLINE MODE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Tiki Karaoke Lounge is designed as an independent media player and party coordination
platform for private home entertainment and family parties.

A. Curated Local Library Mode (Downloader Lock):
   - Easily enabled in Host Remote -> Host Settings.
   - Shuts down online stream search/downloading, restricting singer requests
     strictly to tracks imported into your local library.
   - Perfect for offline environments, cabin parties, or keeping the playlist curated.

B. TikiIQ Smart Importer & CompuHost Migration:
   - Launch "Tiki Karaoke Smart Importer" directly from the desktop Control Hub,
     Setup Wizard, Host Remote Settings, or your Windows Start Menu.
   - 1-Click CompuHost Migration: Instantly auto-detects and imports your CompuHost
     V3/V4/V5 SongList.tps database into Tiki Karaoke with verified artist/title tags.
   - Smart YouTube & Filename Sanitizer: Automatically strips out messy YouTube tags
     (e.g., "[Karaoke Version]", "Instrumental"), autocorrects release years, and scans
     MP4, MKV, WebM, MP3+CDG, and ZIP archives into your local offline crates.
