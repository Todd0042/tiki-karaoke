🌺 Intended Purpose & The Two Distinct Copyright Permissions

Private Home Entertainment: Tiki Karaoke is designed and licensed for private personal use, family gatherings, and home living room parties. Private, non-commercial performances among family and social acquaintances do not require public performance licensing.

Commercial Venues & Public Performances: When operating in a commercial venue (bar, restaurant, lounge, festival, or ticketed event), copyright law requires two distinct rights to be in place:

1. Master & Synchronization Rights (The Media File): Conveys the right to own and play the recorded video track. You obtain this by purchasing legal video files from authorized stores and video pools on the left.
2. Public Performance Rights (The Venue License): Conveys the right for music to be played or performed publicly. Venues obtain this through annual blanket licenses from the Performing Rights Organizations (PROs) on the right.

📺 Commercial Music Video Display Rules
Personal consumer accounts (such as standard YouTube, Spotify, or Apple Music) do not convey rights for commercial public video display in bars or restaurants. For original artist music videos, venues subscribe to licensed commercial video pools (such as Promo Only).
