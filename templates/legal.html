🌴 About Tiki Karaoke & Media Guide

Turnkey Bring-Your-Own-Media player and party coordination system.

🌺 Bring Your Own Media (BYOM) Architecture

Tiki Karaoke Lounge is an independent media player designed for private living room entertainment, family parties, and friendly sing-alongs. The application itself does not host, bundle, or distribute any copyrighted music files.

You bring your personal tracks, and Tiki Karaoke provides dynamic singer queueing, real-time stage lyrics, interactive smartphone song requests via QR code, and retro tropical synthwave visuals.

📂 Supported Media Formats

🎬 Video Karaoke
.mp4, .mkv, .webm video files with embedded audio and synchronized on-screen lyric text.
📀 Karaoke CD (CDG)
Classic .cdg subcode graphics paired with .mp3 audio, or packaged inside .zip archives.
🎤 Sing-Along & BGM
Music videos and sing-along tracks for automatic intermission playback when the singer queue is idle.
