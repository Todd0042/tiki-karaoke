🌴 1. TV Stage & Display Setup

The TV Stage runs at /stage on your main TV or projector. Between songs, it displays the room QR code, counts down intermission, and announces who is Up Next so singers can get to the microphone.

⚡ 2. Live Playback & Stage Controls
  • ⏯️ Pause / Play: Instantly pause or resume video playback on the TV screen.
  • ⏭️ Skip Track: Fade out and skip the current song if the singer finishes early or steps out.
  • ⚡ Start Stage Now: Bypass the intermission countdown and start the next song immediately.
  • +15s Timer: Add 15 extra seconds if a singer needs more time getting to the mic.
🎬 3. BGM Video Autopilot & Crate
  • Autopilot: When the queue runs empty, official music videos automatically play on the TV screen.
  • Room Pulse & 90-min Cooldown: BGM matches the eras/genres sung tonight, and automatically bars songs sung within the last 90 minutes so the party never feels repetitive.
  • Private Preview: In the Crate or YouTube Search tabs, tap 👁️ Preview to privately inspect videos on your phone before stage playback.
🎛️ 4. Audio DSP (Key Pitch & Vocal Cut)
  • Key / Pitch Shift: Adjust pitch by ±1 to ±6 semitones so any song matches a singer's vocal range.
  • Vocal Attenuation: Center-channel filter suppresses guide vocals on tracks with backing singers.
🎙️ 5. Mic Ducking, Soundboard & Voice MC
  • 🎤 Hold to Duck BGM: Press and hold while speaking into the room microphone. TV music drops to 5% volume so your voice cuts through loud and clear.
  • TTS Virtual Announcer: Automatically announces the next singer over the TV sound system.
  • Soundboard: Trigger Air Horn, Applause, Rimshot, or Tropical Bird to hype the room.
👥 6. Fair Queue & Singer Checkout
  • Fair Round-Robin: The queue automatically balances stage time across singers so no single person monopolizes the microphone.
  • Singer Checkout: In the Singer Roster, tap any singer to choose "Just Clear Songs (No-Show)" to purge their queue, or "Singer Left Party (Check Out & Free Name)" to release their profile.
💾 7. Storage Profiles & 1-Tap Save
  • Profiles: Set in Setup: "I'm a Pro" (1080p HD cache), "I'm Inept" (compact 480p), or "Live Only" (0 MB disk / direct stream).
  • 1-Tap Save: In the Songbook / History tab, tap ⬇️ Save on any streamed song to permanently save it to local storage. Tap 🎬 BGM to convert any crowd favorite into an official BGM music video!
