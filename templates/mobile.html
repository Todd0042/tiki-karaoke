LIVE DJ SOUNDBOARD
🌴 1. How the TV Stage Works

The main stage runs at /stage on your TV. Between songs, it plays ambient lounge BGM, counts down 20s, and shows who is Up Next so they can get to the microphone.

⚡ 2. Playback & Stage Controls
  • ⏯️ Pause / Play: Pause or resume video playback on the TV screen.
  • ⏭️ Skip Track: Skip the current song if the singer finishes early or steps out.
  • ⚡ Start Stage Now: Skip the 20s intermission countdown and start the next song immediately.
  • +15s Timer: Add 15 extra seconds if the singer needs time getting to the mic.
🎙️ 3. Mic Ducking & Voice MC
  • 🎤 Hold to Duck BGM: Press & hold while talking into your room microphone. The TV background music instantly ducks to 5% volume so guests hear your voice loud and clear.
  • TTS Virtual Announcer: When checked, the TV automatically speaks each singer's name before their song. Turn it off if you introduce singers yourself!
🔊 4. Live DJ Soundboard

Tap Air Horn, Applause, Rimshot, or Tropical Bird to trigger real-time sound effects out of the TV speakers to hype up great performances.

📜 5. Managing the Queue

Check the Queue tab at the bottom anytime. The fair round-robin queue prevents single-singer hoarding. As Host, you can remove songs or tap ▶️ Play Now to promote any track to the top of the queue.
