🌐 2. Remote Access & SSL / HTTPS
Choose how singers and the stage connect across devices or over the internet:
Cloudflare Named Tunnel Setup:
1. Go to one.dash.cloudflare.com → Networks → Tunnels.
2. Create a tunnel (name it anything you like, e.g. your lounge name) and select cloudflared.
3. Add a Public Hostname pointing to HTTP 127.0.0.1:8000.
Tailscale Funnel Setup:
Ensure Tailscale is installed (curl -fsSL https://tailscale.com/install.sh | sh) and Funnel is enabled in your admin console.
DuckDNS + Caddy Setup:
Register at duckdns.org and forward router ports 80 & 443 to this machine.
📶 Local Access Point / Hotspot Credentials
Optional
If you are hosting via your device's local Wi-Fi hotspot (e.g. at an outdoor party, tailgate, or off-grid cabin), enable this to display a "Join Wi-Fi" QR code on the TV stage screen so guests can connect directly. If guests are already on your home/venue Wi-Fi network, keep this disabled.
💾 3. Storage & Media Library
Choose where downloaded video files, playlists, and database are saved. Perfect for using a dedicated secondary NVMe SSD or storage drive (e.g. C:\Karaoke, D:\Karaoke, or ~/karaoke).
Live MP3 performance recordings are permanently organized inside by each singer's persistent cookie ID (tracks stay linked even if singers change their nickname).
📂 Already have karaoke tracks or CompuHost?
Launch the Smart Importer to sanitize messy YouTube filenames, autocorrect release years, or migrate your CompuHost SongList.tps library directly.

Leave unchecked to open in a standard movable window with minimize/maximize controls (Recommended for easy setup and single-monitor testing).

🔊 5. Audio Output Device Selection
Select where stage audio and music will play (e.g. HDMI out to your TV / AV receiver, PA mixer, or external speakers).
Live Signal:
⚠️ Bluetooth / HDMI-TV detected. These outputs have 80–300ms of OS-level audio delay. Singers will hear a noticeable echo in their voice with Native or Hardware Direct modes.
Switch to Browser WebRTC mode above, or use a wired output (USB audio interface, 3.5mm, or PA mixer) for the stage monitors.
